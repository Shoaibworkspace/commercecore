CommerceCore original sample artwork

All SVG illustrations in this directory were authored for CommerceCore using
original vector shapes. No third-party photographs, logos or artwork were used.

These illustrations are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They depict imaginary demonstration goods. They do not certify the appearance,
availability, safety or properties of any physical product.

The WebP photographs in photos/ are separately AI-generated demonstration
assets made for this project with the built-in image-generation tool. See
photos/provenance.json and photos/expanded-provenance.json for prompts and
asset metadata. The SVG-specific CC0 dedication above does not describe the
legal status of generated photographs. Photographs show imaginary sample
goods, not certified physical inventory or branded merchandise.
